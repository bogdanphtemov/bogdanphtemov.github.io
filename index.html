Projects

Command_line_tools

This is my first personal project, initiated when I was just beginning to explore software development. Originally designed as a suite of simple command-line utilities—ranging from system cleanup to automated repository creation—it has since shifted toward machine learning and artificial intelligence.

Currently, the project consists of a set of CLI-based models built entirely in pure Python, implementing ML algorithms completely from scratch without relying on libraries like scikit-learn. The codebase is thoroughly documented, with detailed guides planned to explain how each algorithm works under the hood. Combined with Python's readability, this makes the project an accessible resource for studying ML mechanics.

My primary goal is to deepen my own understanding of AI and machine learning. Over time, I plan to develop this into a learning-focused alternative to scikit-learn that helps others study ML fundamentals from the ground up.

LifeQuest

Currently on hold, this project was an experiment in building an entire application purely through artificial intelligence alongside another developer. Because of a significant gap in our relative experience levels and the fact that the project fell outside either of our primary specializations, we used AI as an abstraction layer to bridge our skills and navigate the domain.

The project was envisioned as a Telegram bot designed to gamify self-improvement on a trust-based system for a broad audience. However, we hit a technical wall with interface design and formatting: the AI models consistently failed to implement the UI according to our configurations and reference examples. We have temporarily set the project aside and may revisit it when time permits.

More About Me

My name is Bogdan Ftemov, and I am a software engineer from Ukraine. I began learning programming at the age of 13, driven by a desire for a career centered on continuous growth—and I certainly made the right choice. Because software engineering demands constant adaptation to new technologies, I cannot imagine myself in any role other than an engineer. I feel fortunate to have discovered my life's path so early, especially when I see peers struggling to choose theirs.

My journey started before AI was advanced enough to write software independently. Looking back, this was a tremendous advantage: it forced me to master computer science fundamentals first. Building that core foundation rather than relying on AI from day one gave me a deep structural understanding of what I am building, which continues to serve me today as I make informed technical decisions.

After establishing my fundamentals, I integrated AI into my workflow to accelerate development without compromising efficiency or code quality. My fascination with human cognition naturally led me to specialize in artificial intelligence and machine learning. My ultimate career ambition is to contribute to the development of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI) and explore brain-computer integration to expand human cognitive potential. Although the heavy emphasis on mathematics, statistics, and data analysis took me by surprise, I thoroughly enjoy every aspect of it. I have been programming for over two years now with no intention of stopping, currently focusing on a primary educational project alongside several experimental builds.

I am deeply committed to personal development. I believe that with disciplined, structured learning, anyone can achieve extraordinary results regardless of their starting point. Modern tools—especially AI—act as force multipliers to expand human potential. It is our responsibility to leverage these tools to tackle increasingly complex problems; using technology merely for convenience without pushing our own limits inevitably leads to stagnation.

Fascinated by neuroscience, human behavior, and the ambition of one day fully simulating the human brain, I also enjoy solving complex puzzles and exploring philosophical ideas.

Glory to Ukraine!
